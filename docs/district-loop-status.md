# District loop — 1 October 2026

`Assets/Alabama/Scenes/DistrictLoop.unity` is the connected driving route. A 917.0-metre closed centreline drives one continuous MeshCollider road, two shoulders, four lane markings, roadside barriers, and runtime lap progress. Its four rounded corners have a 25-metre centreline radius and are sampled densely enough for smooth wheel contact. It expands the industrial avenue into a second factory street and two shorter cross streets with original modular warehouses, trees, lamps, cranes, and turn markers. The static visual review and straight handling course remain separate scenes.

The road geometry is generated from one shared centreline, so visuals, collision, markings, barrier placement, and route progress use the same authored path. The road and markings are stored as project assets with stable Unity metadata. The road and material configuration are original CC0 work; the scene combines the independently licensed E46, Poly Haven resources, and original industrial kit. Details and hashes are in `docs/assets.json`.

Unity Play Mode tests raycasted the road collider at 15-metre intervals across both driving lanes. A guided controller completed a full 917.0-metre lap in **47.2 simulated seconds**, staying in the corridor and near road height. This verifies that the car can traverse all four corners with repeatable inputs; it does not establish a best lap time or subjective handling quality. All **7 Edit Mode and 6 Play Mode tests** passed. A strict Windows development build succeeded at `builds/district-loop/Alabama.exe`.

The in-engine review images are `artifacts/LoopCapture/chase.png`, `return-street.png`, and `layout.png` (local outputs excluded from Git). The top-down layout has fog disabled solely to make the geometry readable. The scene still needs an interactive driving review, more varied cross-street architecture and surface wear, traffic/audio, and comparison against the reference image. The 1080p benchmark, 30-minute automated drive, and render-cap comparison are now recorded in [qualification status](qualification-status.md); they do not constitute visual approval or subjective handling validation.
