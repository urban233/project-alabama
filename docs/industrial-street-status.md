# Industrial street visual review — 1 October 2026

The first dressed corridor is in `Assets/Alabama/Scenes/IndustrialStreet.unity`. It uses the E46 race-study prefab and a 250-metre straight road with factory frontages, fences, Jersey barriers, two tiers of raised roadway, port cranes, lamps, signs, distant city massing, and three seeded autumn tree variants. The camera is fixed behind the car at a review position. Sun, sky, fog, ambient light, and an ACES grade define a repeatable late-afternoon appearance.

The environment kit has 13 Blender modules and 17,536 library triangles. FBX models retain metre scale and are remapped to explicit URP materials; Unity prefabs have a consistent +Z front. The original `.blend` is retained and all source/export licenses and hashes are recorded in `docs/assets.json`. The kit and lettering are CC0. The E46 remains CC BY-SA 3.0; the Poly Haven sky and asphalt remain CC0.

Verification commands are in `README.md`. `StreetVerify` found 356 scene roots, including the car, 35 trees, 84 barriers, 18 warehouses, and five bridge deck modules. The generated 1920 × 1080 chase and overview images are `artifacts/StreetCapture/chase.png` and `artifacts/StreetCapture/overview.png`; they are local review artifacts excluded from Git. Unity's 7 Edit Mode tests and 1 Play Mode test passed, and the strict Windows development build succeeded at `builds/industrial-street/Alabama.exe`. This confirms import, scene structure, render capture, and build; it does not measure frame rate.

This is an early visual proof. Compared with the screenshot, the street still needs denser city layers, more refined surface wear, stronger warm/cool lighting separation, a final car livery, traffic, a polish pass on signs and props, and art-direction acceptance. No driving, police, HUD, audio, route loop, or measured 60 FPS claim is included at this stage. The next milestone is the production-quality limited-scope handling proof on this corridor.
